Boxing 2026.10.15

三个日常使用中的问题修复：连接线串盒、暗色模式切换与刷新保持、演示页右键退回。

## 这版你能感觉到的变化

### 修复
- **连接线不再串到其他盒子** — 同一大盒内两个小盒子之间的连线只出现在所属页面；删除源线后不再残留副本。跨大盒的小盒子连线数据会保留但暂不绘制（控制台会有提示）。
- **暗色模式可以稳定切回日间** — 从暗色切回日间不再需要新建标签页；刷新或同步之后暗色设置保持。
- **演示页右键可以退回盒子页** — 演示页不再弹出首次引导浮层挡住操作；浮层也可以按 Esc 关闭。

### 内部
- 新增连接渲染面与暗色单宿主两项架构决策。
- 治理：豁免台账撤除；工作日志改为本地未追踪。

## English

Boxing 2026.10.15

Three fixes from daily use: connection lines leaking between boxes, dark mode switching back and surviving a refresh, and right-click back from the demo.

## What you will notice

### Fixed
- **Connection lines no longer leak into other boxes** — a line between two small boxes in the same large box now shows only on that box's page, and deleting the source line no longer leaves copies behind. Lines between small boxes in different large boxes are kept in your data but not drawn (a console warning is logged).
- **Dark mode switches back reliably** — switching from dark back to light no longer needs a new tab, and dark mode survives a refresh or a sync.
- **Demo right-click returns to the box page** — the demo no longer shows the first-run tour overlay that blocked clicks; the overlay can also be closed with Esc.

### Internal
- Architecture decisions: connection surface resolution and single dark-mode host.
- Governance: waiver ledger removed; work logs are local-only (untracked).
