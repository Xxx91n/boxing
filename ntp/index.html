Step 1 of 3

Add your first box

Double-click anywhere on the canvas, or click the + button in the toolbar, to create a large box. Large boxes group related small boxes and bookmarks.

📦
Step 2 of 3

Nest boxes inside

Open a large box, then click + to add small boxes inside it. Each small box holds a list of bookmarks and can be reordered by drag.

⚙
Step 3 of 3

Sync across devices

Open Settings → Sync to connect a WebDAV server. Your boxes sync across browsers and tabs; data is backed up safely with timestamp-based two-way sync.
